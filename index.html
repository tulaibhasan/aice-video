(01)Studio

Show us any edit. We'll make your footage look like it.

Most editors say they'll match your reference and then do their own thing. We work it out shot by shot: how long each cut is, when the captions pop, how the camera moves, which transitions are used, what the colour looks like and where each sound effect hits. We write that down as a spec and rebuild it on your footage. The look stays the same and the content is yours.

Same process for clinics, creators, brands, teachers and kids' channels. English, Urdu, or both.

(04)Process

Brief to
posted.

01

Brief

Send the reference, your raw clips, logo and fonts. We confirm the exact wording and anything we can't make ourselves.

02

Breakdown

The reference is split into sections and written up as a spec: pacing, captions, motion, look and sound.

03

Build

We cut it, then build the motion graphics and 3D in code, then add captions, music and sound effects.

04

Check

Each section is compared with the reference and fixed until it matches.

05

Deliver

A ready-to-post file plus a small preview for your phone. Each revision is a new version (v2, v3), so nothing gets overwritten.
